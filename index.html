Room Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Room Drop is an app for sending files between your own devices (phones, PCs and laptops) over your local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: Room Drop does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

1. Files you send and receive

Files travel directly from one of your devices to another over your local Wi-Fi network. They are never uploaded to the internet, to our servers or to any third party — Room Drop has no servers. Every transfer is encrypted. Received files are saved on your device (on Android in Download/Room Drop) and stay under your control.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, Room Drop keeps a small amount of information on your own device. None of it is sent to us:

  • The device name you choose (for example "Abdul-Phone") and your settings.
  • A list of devices you have paired with, and a security certificate used to encrypt transfers.
  • A transfer history (file names, sizes and where they were saved), limited to the last 200 transfers. You can clear it at any time in the app.

Uninstalling the app removes this information. Files you received are not deleted.

3. Information shared with other devices on your network

While Room Drop is open, it announces your chosen device name, device type (phone, PC or laptop) and a technical device identifier on your local Wi-Fi network so your other devices can find it. This information never leaves your local network. Only devices you have paired with using a PIN can send you files.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No collection of personal information, location, contacts or usage data.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

5. Permissions

  • Network and Wi-Fi access – to find your devices and transfer files on your local network.
  • Notifications – to show transfer progress.
  • Foreground service and keeping the device awake – so a transfer you started keeps running when the screen turns off.

Room Drop only reads files you choose to send, and only writes files you accept.

6. Children

Room Drop does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
